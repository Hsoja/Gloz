Privacy policy

What we collect

When you create an account we store your name, your email address and a one-way hash of your password. When you save a home we store which listing you saved and when. Nothing else is collected.

What we don't do

Gloz has no advertising, analytics or third-party scripts, and it never sells or shares your data. Your browser only stores two cookies: one that keeps you signed in and one that protects forms against forged requests.

Security logs

To detect abuse, the server records events such as failed log-ins. These records contain a salted hash of your IP address, never the address itself, your email or your password.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete your account. Deleting it from the account page removes your profile, your saved homes and every active session immediately.

Your rights

In Canada, PIPEDA gives you the right to access and correct your personal information. Because this is a demo with no real users, there is no contact address for requests.
