About Gloz

Gloz is a portfolio project: a property listings site built with Flask, designed to show a complete, secure web app from the database to the page.

What it does

  • Search homes by city, type, bedrooms and price, filtered on the server
  • Create an account and save homes to compare them later
  • Change your password or delete your account and data at any time

How it protects you

  • Passwords hashed with Argon2id, never stored or logged in plain text
  • Session cookies that scripts can't read, with CSRF protection on every form
  • Rate limits and a temporary lock after repeated failed log-ins
  • A strict Content Security Policy, with no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers

About the listings

Every home on Gloz is fictional. Names, prices and descriptions were written for this demo, and the photos are free stock images from Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay.
